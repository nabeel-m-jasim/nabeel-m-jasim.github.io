Anomalous Pressure Dependence of the Charge Density Wave and Fermi Surface Reconstruction in BaFe₂Al₉

Physical Review Letters — 2025

Authors: Nabeel M. Jasim et al.

This work investigates the evolution of charge density wave order and electronic structure under applied pressure in the three-dimensional quantum material BaFe₂Al₉.

The study reveals anomalous pressure dependence associated with Fermi surface reconstruction, providing new insight into the interplay between lattice effects and electronic ordering in quantum materials.

Research contributions:

  • Experimental investigation of quantum material behavior
  • Analysis of electronic phase transitions
  • Study of pressure-controlled Fermi surface evolution
