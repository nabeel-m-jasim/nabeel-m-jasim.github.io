Research

Experimental Condensed Matter Physics | Quantum Materials

My research focuses on understanding and controlling emergent quantum phenomena in materials through a combination of experimental measurements and first-principles computational approaches.

Experimental Condensed Matter Physics

During my undergraduate research, I developed extensive experience in experimental condensed matter physics, including magnetic, structural, optical, and nanoscale characterization of advanced materials.

SQUID Magnetometry

Extensive experience operating SQUID magnetometers (MPMS) for magnetic susceptibility measurements and investigation of electronic phases in quantum materials.

Materials Characterization

Experience with:

• SEM-EDS elemental analysis
• X-ray diffraction (XRD)
• Raman spectroscopy
• UV-Vis spectroscopy
• Atomic force microscopy (AFM)

Optical Instrumentation

Designed and developed custom optical measurement systems for experimental physics applications, including instrument design, alignment, and scientific data acquisition.

Quantum Materials Research

Charge Density Waves and Fermi Surface Reconstruction

My research investigates electronic ordering phenomena in quantum materials, particularly charge density waves (CDWs), Fermi surface reconstruction, and the relationship between lattice dynamics and electronic structure.

I contributed to the study of:

  • Pressure-dependent charge density wave behavior in BaFe₂Al₉
  • Electronic reconstruction and Fermi surface evolution
  • Mechanisms driving unconventional quantum phases

Computational Quantum Materials — NYU Abu Dhabi

Currently conducting research at New York University Abu Dhabi (NYUAD), using first-principles computational methods to investigate how mechanical strain controls electronic and topological states in quantum materials.

Density Functional Theory (DFT)

Electronic structure calculations, band structure analysis, and prediction of material properties.

Strain Engineering

Studying how mechanical deformation modifies electronic phases and topological properties.

Quantum Phase Control

Exploring routes to manipulate emergent states through external parameters such as strain and pressure.
