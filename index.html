Physical Review Letters Author | Quantum Materials Researcher

Nabeel M. Jasim is an emerging condensed matter physicist and one of the youngest researchers to co-author a publication in Physical Review Letters (PRL), the flagship journal of the American Physical Society and one of the most prestigious physics journals in the world. PRL has published many landmark discoveries recognized by Nobel Prizes in Physics and remains one of the leading venues for transformative research in fundamental physics. His research focuses on quantum materials, charge density waves, Fermi surface reconstruction, and strain-controlled topological states.

During my undergraduate studies, I co-authored a Physical Review Letters publication on Fermi surface reconstruction and anomalous charge density wave behavior in BaFe₂Al₉.

My research combines experimental techniques including SQUID magnetometry, materials characterization, and optical instrumentation with first-principles computational methods to understand and engineer quantum states in materials.

Research Profile

Currently at New York University Abu Dhabi (NYUAD), I investigate how mechanical strain controls electronic and topological states in quantum materials using density functional theory (DFT).

Experimental Physics

SQUID magnetometry (MPMS), SEM-EDS, XRD, Raman spectroscopy, UV-Vis spectroscopy, AFM, and custom optical instrumentation.

Quantum Materials

Charge density waves, Fermi surface reconstruction, topological states, and electronic phase transitions.

Computational Physics

Density functional theory, electronic structure calculations, and strain engineering of quantum materials.
